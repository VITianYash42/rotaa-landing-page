How Rotaa works

Rotaa takes your team’s availability and turns it into a rota that everyone can see on their phone.

  1. Add your team

    Invite staff by link. They enter the days they can work and any time off.

  2. Build the week

    Pick shift times, assign people, and watch hours and cost update as you go.

  3. Publish and relax

    Staff are notified instantly. Swaps and changes go through you, and the rota stays up to date.
